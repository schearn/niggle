For the big ones

Sometimes we build more than an internal fix: a brand-new product with real commercial legs, the kind of thing that could one day be sold or licensed on. If we create something like that together, and only that new product, I take a 10% share if it's ever sold or licensed to someone else. Your business stays entirely yours: your day-to-day, your operations, and any tool that just makes your work run smoother are 100% yours, always. This only ever touches a new product we originate together, it's there because the free groundwork and the original idea help make it worth buying, and we agree it in plain English before a line of code. If it flies, we both win.

The maths is simple: the time, effort and daily headache you claw back pays the fix off fast, then keeps paying long after I've handed you the keys. That's ROI you can feel by Friday.

common questions

What does Niggle build?

Small, bespoke internal tools, built for exactly how your team works: booking systems, quote builders, intake and triage bots, ops dashboards, client portals, data plumbing between systems, web apps, and one-off AI products that didn't exist before. If your team does it, I can usually build a tool for it.

Who is behind Niggle?

Just me, Sam Hearn. I'm based in Bristol and work with small and medium businesses across the UK. I've spent years leading digital teams and overseeing platform builds, so you deal directly with the person who builds the thing, start to finish.

How much does it cost?

The first chat, a deep dive, and a fully branded prototype are free, before you pay anything. After that my day rate is £550, and a typical build runs five to ten days spread across a few weeks. I price against what the problem is costing you, so the fix pays for itself.

How fast can it be built?

Fast. Most builds land in days, not months, because the scope is small and deliberate: we agree the must-haves for a production-ready first version and park the nice-to-haves for later phases.

Do I own what you build?

Yes, completely. It's yours, with no lock-in, and I train your team to run it. Ongoing support is there if you want it, for an agreed fee, but it's never forced.

What won't Niggle take on?

Anything heavy on GDPR-critical data, secure logins at scale, or massive file storage. That's where I point you to a specialist and tell you straight, rather than leave a half-built risk with your name on it.
