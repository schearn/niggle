Bespoke used to cost a fortune. Now it doesn't.

A tool built just for you used to mean a five-figure budget and a six-month wait, so most teams settled for off-the-shelf software that almost fit. The way I build now, a tool made exactly for your workflow costs a fraction of that, and lands in days. For the first time, bespoke is the cheaper option.
